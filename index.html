Market truths

  1. At ≤A$17,100 (US$12,000) the 2023+ market is effectively one car — the MG ZS Excite with 32–60k km.
  2. Genuinely low-km 2023+ cars under A$20k are dealer demo or pre-rego stock, not privately-driven cars.
  3. No brand-new SUV exists under ~A$23k — cheapest new is the 2026 MG ZS Vibe at A$22,990 intro, then Chery Tiggo 4 Urban A$23,990 and Mahindra XUV 3XO A$23,990. Stretching to new costs only ~A$3,000–4,000 above the demo cars above, but buys a full warranty from zero km instead of ~4 years remaining.
  4. carsales "84 results"-style counts mix used, demo, and new-on-order stock — the real used count at any given price is much smaller.

Step-by-step checklist

Pre-departure — now through August 2026
  • A September arrival means this phase is ~6 weeks long, starting now. Bank account, funds transfer, IDP from AAA, and the Carbar application docs (passport, US license, 90 days of bank statements) should all be moving in August.
  • Open an AU bank account — CBA, Westpac, or NAB all support pre-arrival account opening for temporary-visa holders (normally available up to 12 months early; you have weeks, so start immediately).
  • Transfer funds via Wise or OFX (~0.4–0.5% cost, ≈ on a transfer) instead of a bank wire (≈ ).
  • AUSTRAC reporting is the receiving bank's job — nothing for Kevin to file.
Arrival — September 2026
  • VIC allows driving on a US license for 6 months after arrival — for a September landing, the conversion deadline is March 2027.
  • Convert to a VIC license — the US is a Recognised Country, so a test-free conversion is likely, but VIC-specific requirements are unconfirmed — check the VicRoads requirements checker.
  • Bridge the gap with a Carbar subscription while license and insurance get sorted.
Purchase — target September–October 2026
  • Run a PPSR check () before paying anyone.
  • Get a pre-purchase inspection (≈ ).
  • The seller provides the roadworthy certificate — a VIC requirement and the seller's cost, not yours.
  • Pay via a domestic transfer from your AU account, not directly from the US.
  • Transfer registration within 14 days of purchase.
  • Pay stamp duty — 4.2% of the purchase price in VIC.
Insurance
  • Get quotes from AAMI, Budget Direct, Youi, NRMA, and broker National Cover.
  • US no-claims history letters are not accepted by AU insurers.
  • A freshly converted license may carry a inexperienced-driver excess — get this in writing before binding cover.
Exit
  • List the car with a 4–8 week sale runway to avoid the rushed-sale discount.
  • Repatriate proceeds via Wise or OFX.
  • Close the AU bank account before departure.
